Privacy Policy for Quran Pak

Quran Pak is an Islamic mobile application designed to provide prayer times, Quran and dua support, Qibla direction, Dhikr counting, and personal app settings. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app may use, how it is processed, and what is not collected.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Quran Pak does not create user accounts and does not require sign-up with your name, email address, phone number, or payment information. We do not sell personal data. The app does not use advertising trackers or third-party ad networks in the core experience.

2. Information Used for App Features

The app may use the following information to provide its core functionality:

  • Device location: Used to calculate prayer times and determine the Qibla direction. This is either obtained from the device's GPS/location services or from a user-selected city.
  • Local app preferences: Settings such as theme, Adhan enable/disable status, notification offset, selected calculation method, and saved city or location are stored on the device.
  • Bookmarks and local saved data: Verses or user choices saved in the app are stored locally on the device using the app's local storage.
  • Internet connectivity: Required to fetch prayer time data and location-related information from external services used by the app.

3. Third-Party Services

Quran Pak uses external services to provide accurate prayer time and location data. These services may receive the location or requested city information when needed:

  • AlAdhan API: Used to fetch prayer timings and related calculations.
  • BigDataCloud: Used for reverse geocoding and city/area lookup for location-based features.

These services are used only to support the app's features such as prayer timings and location name lookup. The app itself does not use your personal data for unrelated marketing or profiling.

4. Notifications and Audio

The app may request notification permission to play Adhan alerts at scheduled prayer times. Exact alarm permission may also be requested on Android devices to ensure timing accuracy. These notifications are for the app's prayer reminder features and are not used for tracking or advertising.

5. Data Storage and Retention

Information used by the app is stored locally on the device, such as saved settings, bookmarks, location preferences, and theme choices. This data remains on the device unless the user clears app data, uninstalls the app, or removes the app's local storage.

6. Children’s Privacy

This app is not directed at children under the age of 13, and it does not intentionally collect personal information from children. If a parent or guardian believes the app has collected information from a child, please contact us and we will review and remove it if necessary.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and operational steps to protect local app data stored on the device. However, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute protection.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect feature changes, legal requirements, or improvements. Updated versions will be posted in the app or on the app store listing where applicable.
